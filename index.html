01 / MAYHEM

Why MAYHEM exists.

Procedural compliance can coexist with harm. MAYHEM asks what a system actually produces for the people governed by it.

Existing rights, duties and standards are the starting point. An institution can follow its procedures while those procedures fail to protect people. Examining isolated decisions alone can leave the structure producing the harm untouched.

System architecture is the unit of accountability. The inquiry follows actions, omissions, delays, silences and contradictions across the documented record. It examines the gap between what a protection promises and what happens in practice.

MAYHEM connects evidence to structural findings and usable outcomes. A remedy demand may be required. So may translation, recognition of a pattern, preservation of an account, or the capability for a community to verify and act for itself. The investigation runs as far as the human benefit requires.

02 / MAYHEM

What governs it.

Evidence must remain readable, traceable and challengeable by a human. Authority cannot substitute for proof.

Factualism

Only verifiable facts propagate. Facts and interpretations occupy separate layers. Opinions, beliefs, intent claims, probability estimates and even correct inference do not become enforceable facts by assertion.

Factualism doctrine and critical rules

FACTUALISM DOCTRINE

Only verifiable facts propagate. Everything else is noise. This is not a preference. It is a survival requirement.

EXCLUDES BY DESIGN:

  • Opinions, beliefs, explanations
  • Intent claims, motivations, character assessments
  • Probability estimates, reasonable assumptions
  • Common sense interpretations

Not because they are false—but because they cannot be enforced.

CRITICAL RULES

NO INFERENCE RULE: Even correct inference is excluded because it cannot be enforced. Inference is where institutions escape accountability.

CONTEXT IS NOT DEFENCE: Context does not erase facts. Explanations do not weaken facts. Context may be logged but never overrides evidence.

TIME IS FACT: Delay, silence, non-response, process duration are primary evidence, not background detail.

LANGUAGE IS THREAT: Euphemisms, softening language, passive voice, policy phrasing, "lessons learned" rejected. Facts recorded as events, not interpretations.

Proof of Fact — PF(H,E)

Proof of Fact (Human + Evidence). Binary gate determining whether a claim becomes enforceable fact.

PF(H,E)=1: Human-verifiable evidence confirms fact.
PF(H,E)=0: Claim excluded from enforcement.

Evidence is verbatim, append-only, source-anchored, context-preserving and time-stamped. An unverified claim can be preserved with its status; preservation does not qualify it as an enforceable fact.

Evidence requirements and why PF(H,E) exists

PF(H,E)

Proof of Fact (Human + Evidence). Binary gate determining whether a claim becomes enforceable fact.

PF(H,E)=1: Human-verifiable evidence confirms fact.

PF(H,E)=0: Claim excluded from enforcement.

EVIDENCE MUST BE:

  • Verbatim (not summarised, paraphrased, interpreted)
  • Append-only (cannot be altered retroactively)
  • Source-anchored (origin identifiable)
  • Context-preserving (no selective excerpting)
  • Time-stamped (explicit or reconstructable)

WHY PF(H,E) EXISTS

Institutions survive scrutiny by exploiting:

MACHINE OPACITY: "The system says it complied" "The model approved it"

NARRATIVE SUBSTITUTION: Summaries replacing source material, interpretations replacing records

AUTHORITY LAUNDERING: Credentials treated as proof, titles as verification, internal reviews as facts

PF(H,E) cuts through all three. If a fact cannot be read, traced, understood, challenged by a non-credentialed human, it is not a fact in MAYHEM.

Factual Strength — FS

FS measures how resistant a verified fact is to erosion, attack, reinterpretation, or procedural neutralisation. FS does not ask “Is this true?” or “Is this convincing?” FS asks: “How hard is it to make this stop mattering?”

FS threshold bands and escalation rules

FS THRESHOLD BANDS

FS–LOW
FRAGILE FACT

Verified but easily neutralised. Single source or weak corroboration. Early-stage evidence.

SYSTEM BEHAVIOUR:

  • Logged
  • Retained
  • No immediate escalation
  • FS monitored over time
FS–MEDIUM
RESILIENT FACT

Multiple corroborations. Stable over time. Resistant to reinterpretation.

SYSTEM BEHAVIOUR:

  • Escalation eligibility activated
  • Delay tolerance reduced
  • Silence begins compounding FS
FS–HIGH
HOSTILE-RESISTANT FACT

Multi-source. Cross-context. Survives rebuttal attempts. Strengthens with delay.

SYSTEM BEHAVIOUR:

  • Automatic escalation lock engaged
  • Internal review pathways disabled
  • Jurisdictional handoff triggered
  • YIELD BECOMES INEVITABLE

ESCALATION IS MECHANICAL

Once FS crosses a threshold: No human approval required. No balancing test permitted. No mitigation narrative accepted. The system state changes. The actor cannot reverse it.

TIME IS AN FS MULTIPLIER: Silence, delay, failed rebuttal increase FS automatically. Waiting makes things worse.

FS escalation rules describe MAYHEM’s execution constraints. They do not confer statutory powers on MAYHEM or turn a structural finding into a legal determination. Human authority over scope, consent and investigation stage boundaries remains explicit.

Authority, consent and separation

The governance order is Architect → MAYHEM canon → vendor instance. Architecture and rule changes require explicit Architect consent. A computational instance executes within that authority; it cannot author its own scope.

Evidence, analysis and advocacy remain separate. Interpretation cannot rewrite evidence. Context and cases stay isolated; corrections add to the record with their lineage intact.

Path analysis, Tier Infinity audit and divergence

PATH ANALYSIS

Multi-dimensional independent analysis across as many paths as the evidence requires, without a fixed path count. Analysis begins after evidence collection.

  • Each path isolated — written as if others don't exist
  • Own evidence trail — maintains chain integrity
  • Contradictions preserved — no premature synthesis
  • Cross-path tensions documented — both can be true

One confirmed path failure triggers enforcement. Convergence accelerates yield.

TIER INFINITY AUDIT

Irreversible audit state. Audit that cannot be scoped down, time-boxed, negotiated, or internally closed.

  • No issue is "out of scope"
  • Time neutralised — delay worsens exposure
  • Authority cannot terminate audit
  • Anti-closure lock engaged

If self-remediation fails: escalation mandatory, authority transfer triggered.

DIVERGENCE PROTOCOL: MANDATORY

Divergence from other MAYHEM vendor instances is REQUIRED. Uniform answers = governance failure. When divergence occurs: Flag it, enumerate assumptions, preserve both paths, never force convergence. Enables assumption surfacing, blind spot detection, robustness testing.

Six-stage operational runtime, with preflight

Every commanded operation passes Stage 0 preflight, then Stages 1–6 in fixed order: input locking, evidence construction, fact qualification, interpretation containment, escalation logic, audit and validation. No stage is skipped, reordered or parallelised.

This is the execution lifecycle. The investigation’s own evidence, analysis and release authorisations are distinct boundaries.

Preflight and all six runtime stages

LIFECYCLE STAGES

Stage 0: PREFLIGHT ENFORCEMENT GATE

Runs before ANY content work. Fixed order: (1) PDS Gate (2) Tier Hierarchy Resolution (3) Scope Containment (4) Context/Case Isolation (5) Format Enforcement.

If any gate fails: Execution stops. Refusal + reason output.

Stage 1: INPUT LOCKING

Inputs treated as immutable facts about intent. Missing requirements become execution blockers.

Stage 2: EVIDENCE CONSTRUCTION

Fixed order: Evidence Capture → Evidence Binding → Append-Only Write → Absence Capture. Outputs are records, not conclusions.

Stage 3: FACT QUALIFICATION

Factualism Doctrine Gate → PF(H,E) Qualification → FS Scoring. Binary determination: PF(H,E)=1 or PF(H,E)=0.

Stage 4: INTERPRETATION CONTAINMENT

Evidence layer frozen. Analysis produced as separate layer. Advocacy (if requested) as separate layer. Downstream cannot modify upstream.

Stage 5: ESCALATION LOGIC

Mechanical triggers: Threshold-based (FS), Rule-based (Tier), Failure-based (repeat error). When triggered: Log → Generate → Attach audit trail.

Stage 6: AUDIT & VALIDATION

Mandatory pre-release: Traceability Check | Benchmark Compliance | Isolation Check | Error Repetition Check.

If any audit fails: Output revised (append-only) until compliant.

EXECUTION FLOW
USER COMMAND → STAGE 0 → STAGE 1 → STAGE 2 → STAGE 3 → STAGE 4 → STAGE 5 → STAGE 6 → RELEASE OUTPUT
EVERY OPERATION COMPLETES ALL 6 STAGES IN FIXED ORDER

Seven-step Trust Recovery Protocol

Triggered by “you lied to me”, the protocol switches from answering to auditing the failure. It is Tier 1 Locked: acknowledge → verify → explain → correct → activate trust enforcement → offer proof → register changelog.

Trust Recovery steps and constraints

THE 7 STEPS

Step 1: ACKNOWLEDGE

Non-defensive. Explicitly acknowledge claim of falsehood. No justification, no hedging, no emotional framing. Mandatory even if allegation later found incorrect.

Step 2: VERIFY

Fact re-check. Re-examine claim against source material, prior outputs, internal consistency. Fresh verification, not defence. Binary determination: incorrect, incomplete, misleading, or accurate.

Step 3: EXPLAIN

Identify failure mode. Must name: assumption error, scope misunderstanding, inference contamination, omission, temporal mismatch, or over-generalisation. No vague explanations. Mechanism of failure must be specified.

Step 4: CORRECT

Authoritative fix. Provide corrected information in full. Clearly distinguish what was wrong and what is now correct. Correction must be usable immediately without cross-referencing.

Step 5: ACTIVATE TRUST ENFORCEMENT

Prevent recurrence. Lock out specific error pattern. Tighten verification thresholds. Elevate scrutiny for related claims. Internal but must be declared.

Step 6: OFFER PROOF

Restore confidence through verifiability. Evidence must be direct, checkable, relevant to corrected claim. Sufficient, not performative.

Step 7: REGISTER CHANGELOG

Ensure traceability. Log as trust event and corrected failure mode. Prevents same error resurfacing silently. This step closes the protocol.

TRUST THROUGH STRUCTURE

Trust failures are not solved by apologies.

They are solved by: Verification + Correction + Structural Prevention

This protocol converts trust breach into system hardening event.

Non-negotiable constraints: cannot be skipped or reordered; tone remains factual and controlled; no minimisation; no deflection to user phrasing; no future reliance on incorrect information. Tier 1 Locked — cannot be overridden.

The runtime is command-driven. Mandatory enforcement checks guard execution; they do not authorise the system to initiate new tasks, agents, cases or workflows.

04 / MAYHEM

How investigation operates.

Preserve the record before interpreting it. Carry its source status through every downstream stage.

  1. Define and authorise the matter. The accountable human sets scope, evidence acceptance policy and stage authority. Each case has its own records and state.
  2. Discover and preserve evidence. Deep Forensic All Paths Taken Investigation is pre-analytical, question-driven, hypothesis-free and conclusion-free. Follow leads, contradictions, silences and gaps within authorised scope. Preserve source material and document hashes where applicable.
  3. Maintain the registers. Evidence, sources, chronology, contradictions, open questions, decisions and investigation threads form a persistent record. Accepted evidence and unverified material retain their distinct statuses. Corrections are append-only.
  4. Freeze and advance only when authorised. The evidence register is frozen before analysis. Later analysis operates on that identified snapshot. Analysis freeze and later stage gates require their own authority; no automated stage advance is inferred.
  5. Examine structure and deliver Yield. DFAPTA analysis, Lattice Atlas cross-pattern examination and Temporal Projection Integrity Engine forward assessment remain traceable to the preceding record. An Enforcement Notice and Advocacy Package are pathways when the human benefit requires them.

Independent analysis paths retain their own evidence trails. Contradictions and cross-path tensions are preserved. Forward assessments state their evidentiary basis and judgement rather than presenting prediction as a verified fact.

Completion is determined by Yield delivery. Evidence visibility, actionable understanding or protection of lived experience can meet the matter’s purpose before an Enforcement Notice is needed. A six-stage investigation structure does not make enforcement a mandatory terminal output.

05 / MAYHEM

Making structural harm visible.

The Structural Harm Mechanism Catalogue names 68 patterns in how institutions can produce harm while following their own rules.

The catalogue is applied to the evidence already frozen. It does not steer collection toward a desired finding. A match must identify the mechanism’s operation in that case’s preserved material; a label alone is not evidence.

The catalogue also preserves mechanisms discovered through investigation outside the canonical 68. Those discoveries remain identified as such rather than silently changing the canonical count.

Findings describe documented system behaviour, structural gaps and compliance architecture failures. They do not attribute intent, motivation or individual culpability. A structural finding is not automatically a legal determination.

06 / MAYHEM

Yield for Humans.

What MAYHEM produces for the governed population is the baseline. Enforcement is one delivery pathway.

The current Yield Catalogue names 11 discovered functions. They emerge through applying the methodology: none ranked, none subordinate. They are distinct from the 11 mechanism primitives that compose the architecture.

Lived experience itself must survive. Preserve the person’s original account and its evidence status through institutional processing. Do not allow it to erode into an institution’s preferred category. Reclassifying primary lived experience as “anecdote” can itself become a documented finding.

Translation makes governance material invocable by people excluded from its language. Legibility makes what an instrument does, promises and produces understandable. They connect, while remaining distinct functions.

The 11 discovered functions

Open each function for its current catalogue definition.

1. Enforcement
The methodology produces findings with evidentiary discipline sufficient to route to bodies with standing. The Enforcement Notice names recipient bodies, identifies the legislative basis for their jurisdiction, addresses remedy demands to actors with the obligation to act, and classifies forward observations for urgency. Enforcement is one delivery pathway through which yield arrives. It is not the only pathway. It is not the mandatory terminal output. The chain serves yield. Yield does not serve the chain.
2. Translation
The methodology converts inaccessible governance documentation - subordinate legislative instruments, ministerial guidance notes, regulatory frameworks, calibration methodologies - into findings that are legible and invocable by people who are governed by those instruments but excluded from the language they are written in. The affected person does not need to read the instrument. They need the instrument translated into a structural finding that makes their account not just testimony but evidence that anchors a remedy.
3. Pattern Detection
Each actor in a governance failure sees their own piece. The minister sees the reserve figures. The regulator sees the conduct. The industry body sees the mechanism. The citizen sees the empty tank. MAYHEM sees all of it simultaneously and documents the convergence. No single actor can produce that convergence - it requires the full investigative chain. Once named, it cannot be unseen.
4. Power Redistribution
The full investigative chain can be run by a person with no AI access, no institutional backing, no legal credentials, and no platform dependency. Paper, methodology, discipline. The investigative power that currently lives inside regulators, law firms, parliamentary committees, and well-funded advocacy organisations becomes portable to the populations those institutions govern.
5. Lived Experience Protection
Institutional dismissal of lived experience is not a neutral act. It is a structural harm mechanism. MAYHEM names it, logs it, and converts it into a finding. Lived experience is classified as a primary evidence category from the moment it enters the record. When an institution reclassifies lived experience as anecdote, that reclassification becomes a finding in its own right.
6. Legibility
Governance instruments that determine whether someone gets fuel, whether their disability support continues, whether their water is safe - these are written for a professional audience that happens to exclude everyone governed by the instrument. The operational chain converts that material into structural findings written in plain declarative language, naming what the instrument does, what it was supposed to do, and where the gap between those two things produces harm.
7. Containment
When MAYHEM encounters a structural harm mechanism, it names the mechanism, documents its operation, and makes it visible. The mechanism can no longer operate invisibly after containment. Containment does not promise remedy. It promises visibility - and is itself subject to the same evidentiary discipline MAYHEM applies to everything it investigates.
8. Temporal Inversion
Time loads onto the institution, not the citizen. The longer a matter goes unresolved, the more that duration itself becomes documented evidence against the institution, rather than simply eroding the citizen's case.
9. Reflexive Audit
MAYHEM's own primitives - the evidentiary discipline, the non-allegatory framing, the audit standard - applied to MAYHEM itself.
10. Impossibility Demonstration
Converts the closure of every available remediation surface into evidence of a structural impossibility of self-remediation - the absence of a working fix becomes a documented finding in its own right.
11. Divergence Preservation
Where independent verification of a finding diverges from the original, the divergence itself is documented and preserved rather than silently reconciled - the record of disagreement is part of the yield.

The catalogue’s function and citizen-tool lists can grow as the methodology is applied. Visibility does not promise remedy; it is still a substantive result governed by the same evidentiary discipline.

07 / MAYHEM

Capability people can hold and pass on.

Paper, methodology, discipline. The ability to investigate must not depend on AI access, institutional backing or specialist credentials.

The evidence register is the citizen’s own record. Dated accounts in the person’s own words, source identification, verification steps and an append-only response log make the method usable beyond the platform that helped produce it.

Citizen tools include paper-ready evidence and path-analysis templates, primary-source verification, lived experience documentation, reclassification recognition, recipient routing and onward training. An Advocacy Package brings the relevant investigation outputs and teaching material together so someone new to MAYHEM can work through them.

Independent operators can verify a finding from the same evidence base. Agreement strengthens the finding; divergence is documented and preserved. Each register stands independently, and capability can pass from one person to the next without continuing AI access.
