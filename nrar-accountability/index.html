Investigation purpose

Examine whether enforcement actions by the NSW Natural Resources Access Regulator lead to verified environmental protection, removal of unlawful commercial advantages and ecological recovery. A fine or conviction is not proof of restoration.

Green Leaf Australia Group — Clybucca

Established enforcement: 2025 NSW Local Court sentencing judgment concerning Green Leaf Australia Group Pty Ltd and Xiuming Lin; combined fines of $295,250. The judgment directed half the fines to the prosecutor. This is an allocation order, not evidence of receipt or budgetary use.

Remediation: Court orders concern dams 4, 5 and 9 and vegetation rehabilitation.

Environmental recovery: UNKNOWN. A publicly verified completion report has not been incorporated into this investigation.

Historical court evidence records a Chinese corporate majority shareholder; nationality or ownership is not evidence of wrongdoing.

Judgment [2025] NSWLC 1

Regulator performance

The NSW Audit Office's 15 June 2026 report, Water management and regulation in NSW, identifies weaknesses in regulatory data and outcome-focused performance reporting. These are institutional accountability issues; they do not prove collusion or that remediation failed in an individual case.

Audit Office report

What must be verified

  1. What unlawful activity was established and when?
  2. What fines, costs and remediation orders were imposed?
  3. Where did fine proceeds go, and were they actually received?
  4. Were remediation plans approved and physical works completed?
  5. Was ecological recovery independently measured?
  6. Was any economic advantage removed?

Evidence classification: Court-established / regulator-reported / independently verified / inference / unknown. Missing public records do not establish noncompliance.
